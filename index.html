Treatment Process

Interactive process model
01

Screening

Removes larger suspended debris before finer treatment stages.

02

Coagulation + Flocculation

Destabilizes fine particles so they can form larger flocs for separation.

03

Sedimentation

Provides a settling stage in the simulated process for formed solids.

04

Sand / Multimedia Filter

Reduces suspended solids and turbidity through depth filtration.

05

Activated Carbon

Represents adsorption-based polishing for selected dissolved organics and taste/odor compounds.

06

Fine / Membrane Polish

Represents a configurable fine-barrier stage for additional particulate reduction.

07

UV Disinfection

Applies a simulated UV exposure stage; effectiveness depends on exposure and water clarity.

08

Final Verification

Checks the treated-water indicators before opening the release gate.

How the simulation decides

TEST→SELECT TREATMENT→REMOVE→RE-TEST→RELEASE / ALERT

AQUANEX is intentionally shown as a closed-loop system: treatment does not automatically mean safe water. The simulated final test must pass before the output valve can open.
